# Product Backlog & Deferred Feature Scope

The following capabilities are explicitly deferred from Phase 6 and tracked for future product iterations:

## Phase 6 Deferred Items

1. **Scheduled & Emailed Reports**: Cron-driven delivery of PDF/XLSX summaries via SMTP/SES to department heads or executive email lists.
2. **Custom Report Builder**: Ad-hoc drag-and-drop query and column designer with customizable formulas.
3. **Saved Report Presets**: Per-user saved filter combinations, custom default column views, and bookmarking.
4. **External BI Connectors**: OData/REST feeds for direct ingestion into PowerBI, Tableau, or Snowflake.
