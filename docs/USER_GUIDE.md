# Employee Portal User Guide

Welcome to the IT Asset Management Employee Self-Service Portal.

---

## 1. Navigating Your Workspace

Upon logging in with your employee email and password, you have access to:

- **My Assets**: View company laptops, monitors, phones, and peripherals currently assigned to you.
- **My Requests**: Request new equipment, software license access, or hardware repairs.
- **Credentials & Access**: Submit and view sensitive credentials (VPN tokens, shared service keys) under explicit organizational consent notice.
- **Offboarding Progress**: When departing the organization, review the status of your exit checklist and download your final signed equipment handover receipt.

---

## 2. Requesting Hardware or Software

1. Navigate to **Requests** -> Click **New Request**.
2. Select request category (`Hardware`, `Software`, `Repair`, or `Access`).
3. Specify justification and required delivery date.
4. Monitor approval status directly from your request dashboard. Realtime updates notify you when your manager or IT department acts on your request.

---

## 3. Submitting Shared Credentials Securely

1. Navigate to **Credentials** -> Click **Add Credential**.
2. A mandatory organizational consent notice appears explaining that credentials stored in the vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and access is audited.
3. Enter credentials; values are transmitted with payload encryption and never stored in plain browser memory.
